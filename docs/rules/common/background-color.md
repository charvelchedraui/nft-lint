# common/background-color

**Severity:** see the rule pack. **Network:** not required

## What it checks

background_color.

## Why it matters

OpenSea expects 6 hex characters and no hash. A leading # is ignored or shown as the wrong color.

## How to fix

Use e8dcc8, not #e8dcc8.

Docs: https://github.com/charvelchedraui/nft-lint/blob/main/docs/rules/common/background-color.md
