# evm/eip2981

**Severity:** see the rule pack. **Network:** not required

## What it checks

EIP-2981 royalties.

## Why it matters

If supportsInterface(0x2a55205a) is false, most marketplaces will not pay the royalty the creator expects. A zero receiver or a fee that disagrees with contractURI is worse.

## How to fix

Implement royaltyInfo. Keep the receiver and basis points aligned with contractURI. Fees above 10% are warned, not rejected.
